問題：次の式を計算せよ

  • (1)の答え：\( \dfrac{\pi}{8}\log 2 \)

    解説：

    point:King Property

    \( I = \displaystyle \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \dfrac{x(\tan^2 x + 1)}{\tan x + 1} dx \) とおく。

    \( \hspace{35px} \begin{eqnarray} I & = & \displaystyle \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \dfrac{(\frac{\pi}{4} - x)(\tan^2 (\frac{\pi}{4} - x) + 1)}{\tan (\frac{\pi}{4} - x) + 1} dx \\ & = & \displaystyle \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \dfrac{(\frac{\pi}{4} - x)\bigg(\bigg( \dfrac{1 - \tan x}{1 + \tan x} \bigg)^2 + 1\bigg)}{\dfrac{1 - \tan x}{1 + \tan x} + 1} dx \\ & = & \displaystyle \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \dfrac{(\frac{\pi}{4} - x)\bigg( \dfrac{2 + 2\tan^2 x}{(1 + \tan x)^2} \bigg)}{\dfrac{2}{1 + \tan x}} dx \\ & = & \displaystyle \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \dfrac{(\frac{\pi}{4} - x)(\tan^2 x + 1)}{\tan x + 1} dx \\ I & = & \dfrac{\pi}{4}\displaystyle \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \dfrac{(\tan^2 x + 1)}{\tan x + 1} dx - I \\ 2I & = & \dfrac{\pi}{4}\left[ \log|\tan x + 1| \right]_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \\ I & = & \dfrac{\pi}{8}\log 2 \end{eqnarray} \)

  • (2)の答え：\( \dfrac{16}{5}x^5 - 2x^4 + \dfrac{4}{3}x^3 - x^2 + x + C \)

    解説：

    point:分子分母共に等比数列

    \( \hspace{35px} \begin{eqnarray} \displaystyle \int \dfrac{256x^8 + 64x^6 + 16x^4 + 4x^2 + 1}{16x^4 + 8x^3 + 4x^2 + 2x + 1} dx & = & \displaystyle \int \dfrac{(4x^2)^5 - 1}{4x^2 - 1} \cdot \dfrac{1}{\dfrac{(2x)^5 - 1}{2x - 1}} dx \\ & = & \displaystyle \int \dfrac{(2x)^5 + 1}{2x + 1} dx \\ & = & \displaystyle \int \dfrac{(-2x)^5 - 1}{(-2x) - 1} dx \\ & = & \displaystyle \int (16x^4 - 8x^3 + 4x^2 -2x + 1) dx　(\because 初項1, 公比-2x の等比数列) \\ & = & \dfrac{16}{5}x^5 - 2x^4 + \dfrac{4}{3}x^3 - x^2 + x + C \end{eqnarray} \)

  • (3)の答え：\( -\log|e^x\cos x + \sin x| \)

    解説：

    point:マイナーな定石置換。 \( \sin^2 x, \cos^2 x \) があったらこの置換を考えてみる。

    \( \hspace{35px} \begin{eqnarray} \displaystyle \int \dfrac{e^x(\tan x - 1) - 1}{e^x + \tan x} dx & = & \displaystyle \int \tan x - \dfrac{e^x + (1 + \tan^2 x)}{e^x + \tan x} dx \\ & = & -\log|\cos x| - \log|e^x + \tan x| \\ & = & -\log|e^x\cos x + \sin x| \end{eqnarray} \)

  • (4)の答え：\( \dfrac{1}{2}\log|\tan^2 x - 1| - \dfrac{1}{2(\tan^2 x - 1)} + C \)

    解説：

    point:分数が出てきたらまずは割れるかどうかを確かめる。

    \( t = \tan x \)とおくと、
    \( \sin^2 x = \tan^2 x\cos^2 x = \dfrac{t^2}{1 + t^2}, dx = \dfrac{1}{1 + t^2} dt \)


    \( \hspace{35px} \begin{eqnarray} \displaystyle \int \dfrac{\tan x\sin^2 x}{(2\sin^2 x - 1)^2} dx & = & \displaystyle \int \dfrac{t\dfrac{t^2}{1 + t^2}}{(2\dfrac{t^2}{1 + t^2} - 1)^2} \dfrac{1}{1 + t^2} dt \\ & = & \displaystyle \int \dfrac{t^3}{(t^2 - 1)^2} dt \\ & = & \displaystyle \int \bigg\{ \dfrac{t}{t^2 - 1} + \dfrac{t}{(t^2 - 1)^2} \bigg\} dt \\ & = & \dfrac{1}{2}\log|t^2 - 1| - \dfrac{1}{2(t^2 - 1)} + C \\ & = & \dfrac{1}{2}\log|\tan^2 x - 1| - \dfrac{1}{2(\tan^2 x - 1)} + C \end{eqnarray} \)

  • (5)の答え：\( \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{18}\pi \)

    解説：

    point:四次以上方程式の因数分解法

    \( x^4 + x^3 + 3x^2 - 2x + 4 = (x^2 + px + q)(x^2 + rx + s) \)とおいて、右辺を展開して係数比較をすると
    \( x^4 + x^3 + 3x^2 - 2x + 4 = (x^2 + 2x + 4)(x^2 - x + 1) \) が得られる。
    \( \hspace{35px} \begin{eqnarray} \displaystyle \int_{0}^{2} \dfrac{dx}{x^4 + x^3 + 3x^2 - 2x + 4} & = & \displaystyle \int_{0}^{2} \dfrac{dx}{(x^2 + 2x + 4)(x^2 - x + 1)} dx \\ & = & \dfrac{1}{9}\displaystyle \int_{0}^{2} \bigg\{ \dfrac{x + 1}{x^2 + 2x + 4} - \dfrac{x - 2}{x^2 - x + 1} \bigg\} dx \\ & = & \dfrac{1}{18}\displaystyle \int_{0}^{2} \bigg\{ \dfrac{2x + 2}{x^2 + 2x + 4} - \dfrac{2x - 1}{x^2 - x + 1} + \dfrac{3}{x^2 - x + 1} \bigg\} dx \\ & = & \left[ \log|x^2 + 2x + 4| - \log|x^2 - x + 1| \right]_{0}^{2} + \dfrac{1}{6}\displaystyle \int_{0}^{2} \dfrac{1}{(x - \frac{1}{2})^2 + \frac{3}{4}} dx \\ & = & \left[ \log\bigg| \dfrac{x^2 + 2x + 4}{x^2 - x + 1} \bigg| \right]_{0}^{2} + \dfrac{1}{6} \cdot \dfrac{1}{\dfrac{3}{4}} \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \displaystyle \int_{-\frac{\pi}{6}}^{\frac{\pi}{3}} d\theta　\bigg( x - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\tan \theta \bigg) \\ & = & (\log 4 - \log 4) + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{9}\bigg\{ \dfrac{\pi}{3} - (-\dfrac{\pi}{6}) \bigg\} \\ & = & \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{18}\pi \end{eqnarray} \)
