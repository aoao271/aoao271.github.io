問題：次の式を計算せよ

  • (1)の答え：\( \dfrac{\pi}{4} - \dfrac{2}{3} \)

    解説：

    point:King Property

    \( I = \displaystyle \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \dfrac{\tan^4 x}{e^x + 1} dx \) とおく。

    \( \hspace{35px} \begin{eqnarray} I & = & \displaystyle \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \dfrac{\tan^4 x}{e^{-x} + 1} dx \\ & = & \displaystyle \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \dfrac{e^x\tan^4 x}{e^x + 1} dx \\ \therefore 2I & = & \displaystyle \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \dfrac{\tan^4 x}{e^x + 1} dx + \displaystyle \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \dfrac{e^x\tan^4 x}{e^x + 1} dx \\ I & = & \dfrac{1}{2}\displaystyle \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \tan^4 x dx \\ & = & \dfrac{1}{2}\displaystyle \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \tan^2 x\bigg( \dfrac{1}{\cos^2 x} - 1 \bigg) dx \\ & = & \dfrac{1}{2}\displaystyle \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \bigg( \dfrac{\tan^2 x}{\cos^2 x} - \dfrac{1}{\cos^2 x} + 1 \bigg) dx \\ & = & \left[ \dfrac{\tan^3 x}{3} - \tan x + x \right]_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}　(\because 偶関数) \\ & = & \bigg( \dfrac{1}{3} - 1 + \dfrac{\pi}{4} \bigg) - 0 \\ & = & \dfrac{\pi}{4} - \dfrac{2}{3} \end{eqnarray} \)

  • (2)の答え：\( \dfrac{x^2}{\log x} + C \)

    解説：

    point:微分結果の予測

    \( t = \log x \)とおくと、\( e^t = x, e^tdt = dx \)


    \( \hspace{35px} \begin{eqnarray} \displaystyle \int \dfrac{x(2\log x - 1)}{(\log x)^2} dx & = & \displaystyle \int \dfrac{e^{2t}(2t - 1)}{t^2} dt \\ & = & \displaystyle \int \dfrac{2te^{2t} - e^{2t}}{t^2} dt \\ & = & \dfrac{e^{2t}}{t} + C \\ & = & \dfrac{x^2}{\log x} + C \end{eqnarray} \)

  • (3)の答え：\( \dfrac{11}{8}\pi \)

    解説：

    point:等比数列の和

    \( \hspace{35px} \begin{flalign} & \displaystyle \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} (\sin 2x\cos x + \sin 2x + 2\sin x)(\sin 2x\cos x - \sin 2x + 2\sin x) dx \\ & = \displaystyle \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \dfrac{2\sin x(1 - \cos^3 x)}{1 - \cos x}\dfrac{2\sin x(1 - (-\cos x)^3)}{1 - (-\cos x)} dx \\ & = 4\displaystyle \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} (1 - \cos^6 x) dx \\ & = 4\bigg( \dfrac{\pi}{2} - \dfrac{5!!}{6!!}\dfrac{\pi}{2} \bigg)　(\because ウォリス積分) \\ & = 2\pi\bigg( 1 - \dfrac{5 \cdot 3 \cdot 1}{6 \cdot 4 \cdot 2} \bigg) \\ & = \dfrac{11}{8}\pi \end{flalign} \)

  • (4)の答え：\( \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{8}\log3 - \dfrac{\pi}{12} \)

    解説：

    point:逆関数っぽい形だと思うべし。

    \( \hspace{35px} \begin{eqnarray} y & = & \log\bigg( \dfrac{1 + \sqrt{1 - 4x^2}}{2x} \bigg) とおくと、 \\ e^y & = & \dfrac{1 + \sqrt{1 - 4x^2}}{2x} \\ 2xe^y - 1 & = & \sqrt{1 - 4x^2} \\ 4x^2e^{2y} - 4xe^y + 1 & = & 1 - 4x^2 \\ (e^{2y} + 1)x^2 - e^yx & = & 0 \\ \therefore x & = & \dfrac{e^y}{e^{2y} + 1} \end{eqnarray} \)

    \( x \) \( \frac{1}{2} \to \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \)
    \( y \) \( 0 \to \log\sqrt{3} \)

    より


    \( \hspace{35px} \begin{eqnarray} \displaystyle \int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}} \log\bigg( \dfrac{1 + \sqrt{1 - 4x^2}}{2x} \bigg) dx & = & \bigg( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\log\sqrt{3} - \frac{1}{2} \cdot 0 \bigg) - \displaystyle \int_{0}^{\log\sqrt{3}} \dfrac{e^y}{e^{2y} + 1} dy \\ & = & \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\log\sqrt{3} - \displaystyle \int_{1}^{\sqrt{3}} \dfrac{1}{t^2 + 1} dt　(\because t = e^y) \\ & = & \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{8}\log3 - \bigg[ \theta \bigg]_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}　(\because t = \tan \theta) \\ & = & \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{8}\log3 - \dfrac{\pi}{12} \end{eqnarray} \)

  • (5)の答え：\( \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\log\bigg| \dfrac{x + \sqrt{x^2 - 1} + 2 -\sqrt{3}}{x + \sqrt{x^2 - 1} + 2 + \sqrt{3}} \bigg| + C \)

    解説：

    point:\( \displaystyle \int \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 + a}} dx \) 型の積分方法を考える。


    \( t = \log(x + \sqrt{x^2 - 1}) \)とおくと、\( dt = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 - 1}} dx \)


    \( \hspace{35px} \begin{eqnarray} また　t & = & \log(x + \sqrt{x^2 - 1}) \\ e^t & = & x + \sqrt{x^2 - 1} \\ e^t - x & = & \sqrt{x^2 - 1} \\ e^{2t} - 2xe^t + x^2 & = & x^2 - 1 \\ e^{2t} + 1 & = & 2xe^t \\ \therefore x & = & \dfrac{e^t + e^{-t}}{2} \\ x + 2 & = & \dfrac{e^t + e^{-t} + 4}{2} \end{eqnarray} \)


    \( \hspace{35px} \begin{eqnarray} \displaystyle \int \dfrac{dx}{(x + 2)\sqrt{x^2 - 1}} & = & \displaystyle \int \dfrac{2}{e^t + e^{-t} + 4} dt \\ & = & \displaystyle \int \dfrac{2e^t}{e^{2t} + 4e^t + 1} dt \\ & = & \displaystyle \int \dfrac{2}{u^2 + 4u + 1} du　(\because u = e^t) \\ & = & \displaystyle \int \dfrac{2}{(u + 2)^2 - (\sqrt{3})^2} du \\ & = & \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\displaystyle \int \bigg\{ \dfrac{1}{(u + 2) - \sqrt{3}} - \dfrac{1}{(u + 2) + \sqrt{3}} \bigg\} du \\ & = & \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\log\bigg| \dfrac{u + 2 -\sqrt{3}}{u + 2 + \sqrt{3}} \bigg| + C \\ & = & \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\log\bigg| \dfrac{e^t + 2 -\sqrt{3}}{e^t + 2 + \sqrt{3}} \bigg| + C \\ & = & \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\log\bigg| \dfrac{x + \sqrt{x^2 - 1} + 2 -\sqrt{3}}{x + \sqrt{x^2 - 1} + 2 + \sqrt{3}} \bigg| + C \end{eqnarray} \)

    ※補足

    積分No.23と積分No.32の(5)の置換方法でも同様に解けるので、興味がある人はその方法でも解いてみてね。
    (積分定数の影響でここでの答えと少し違うかもしれないから、定積分にして値が合ってるか検算してみてね)
